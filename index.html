About

I am a master’s student at UIUC, building on a background in econometrics and software development at the University of Sydney. My current technical work explores numerical reasoning in language models and evaluation of tool-using agents.

I am developing a research direction in embodied AI and robotics, with particular interest in how learned models support reliable decisions and actions. I hope to pursue PhD research in this area in the future.

Research interests

Directions I hope to investigate through future research:

Embodied models & robot learning

Vision-language-action (VLA) models, world models, and multimodal representations for robotic manipulation, including tactile sensing.

Reliable decisions & evaluation

Reinforcement learning for decision-making, safety constraints, and diagnostic evaluation of learned policies and agents.
